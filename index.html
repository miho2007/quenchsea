Three stages between the ocean and your cup.

Each pull of the lever pushes seawater through the same path, in this order.

1

Pre-filter

A triple pre-filtration stage strips out sediment and particles before the water reaches the membrane.

2

Reverse osmosis

The hydraulic pump pressurises the water through a 0.01 micron membrane that rejects salt and bacteria.

3

Carbon polish

An activated carbon filter improves taste and smell, so the water is pleasant to drink.

Questions, answered.

Does it need power?

No. You pull a telescoping lever by hand, and a built-in hydraulic pump does the rest.

How fast does it make water?

The company claims up to 3 litres an hour in ideal conditions, which is about 20 minutes per litre. Real output depends on how steadily you pump.

Is the water safe to drink?

The company says the output meets World Health Organization standards. The reverse osmosis membrane rejects salt and bacteria, and a carbon filter finishes the taste.

Who makes it?

QuenchSea is a London-based startup owned by Dubai energy firm Hydro Wind Energy, led by CEO Lee King.

When does it ship?

The original Indiegogo campaign targeted deliveries from February 2021. This page is a homework concept, so nothing is shipped.
